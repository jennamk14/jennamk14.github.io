\section{Research Interests}
% suggestion from ESA: looking for keywords to help committees understand my research.
% computer science often has these headers, but i could break down into keywords: Cyber-physical systems, edge AI, distributed intelligence, adaptive autonomy, cross-modal sensing, environmental monitoring, ecology, biodiversity, coastal resilience, climate resilience
\textbf{Cyber-Physical Systems for Adaptive Environmental Sensing at the Far Edge}

I build \textbf{cyber-physical systems} for autonomous environmental sensing in dynamic, resource-constrained field settings. My research advances \textbf{distributed intelligence}, \textbf{edge AI}, and \textbf{adaptive autonomy} to coordinate heterogeneous sensors and robots, enabling \textbf{cross-modal systems} to adapt what, where, and when they observe. I deploy these systems across \textbf{ecology}, \textbf{biodiversity monitoring}, and \textbf{coastal and climate resilience}, developing the foundations for persistent, adaptive observation of complex environmental systems.
