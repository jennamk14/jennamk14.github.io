About

I am a PhD candidate studying computer science and engineering at The Ohio State University, with an expected graduation date of Spring 2026.

Currently looking for Fall 2026 opportunities!

I develop agentic, multi-modal autonomous remote sensing systems that use edge AI to enable adaptive sampling for environmental monitoring.

My work creates intelligent agents that dynamically adjust their sensing strategies in real-time based on environmental conditions and data patterns. These systems combine computer vision, robotics, and edge computing to make autonomous decisions in the field, optimizing data collection for ecological research and conservation applications. Through interdisciplinary collaboration, I bridge cutting-edge AI capabilities with practical environmental monitoring needs.

I am fortunate to be coadvised by Dr. Christopher Stewart and Dr. Tanya Berger-Wolf, and contribute to the ICICLE and Imageomics Research Institutes at OSU.
